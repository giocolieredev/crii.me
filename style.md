
  **Style Overview:**

  - **Color Scheme:** Brutalism is characterized by a palette of neutral colors. For this design, we will use orange and 
  black as the primary colors. Orange can represent energy and dynamism while black signifies strength and reliability.  

  - **Typography:** Sans-serif fonts are preferred in brutalist designs to maintain a clean and modern look. Consider    
  using Google Fonts like "Roboto" or "Open Sans".

  - **Layout:** The layout should be minimalistic with a focus on functionality. Avoid cluttered layouts; instead,       
  organize elements into logical sections.

  - **Elements:**
    - **Backgrounds:** Use solid colors for backgrounds, focusing on orange and black.
    - **Borders:** Minimal borders or none at all.
    - **Headers:** Large, bold headers to emphasize content.
    - **Links:** A consistent color (e.g., orange) for internal and external links.

  - **Typography:**
    - Headings: Bold and slightly larger in size
    - Body Text: Regular weight

  **Design Rules:**

  1. **Color Balance:** Maintain a balance of orange and black throughout the website.
  2. **Font Consistency:** Use a single, clean font for all text.
  3. **White Space:** Utilize plenty of white space to avoid overwhelming users with too much information.
  4. **Alignment:** Align elements in a clean, grid-like pattern.

  **Branding:**

  - The brand should be represented consistently across all pages and sections of the website.
  - Use orange and black as primary colors for branding materials like logos, buttons, and other interactive elements.   

  This design style aims to create a modern, professional look that is both functional and visually appealing. By        
  following these guidelines, you can ensure that your website meets the brutalist aesthetic while maintaining a strong  
  brand identity.
